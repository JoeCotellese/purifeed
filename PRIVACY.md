# Purifeed — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 7, 2026_

Purifeed is a browser extension that hides suggested posts, promoted posts, the
LinkedIn News module, and the puzzles module from your LinkedIn feed. This policy
explains what the extension does and does not do with your information.

## The short version

Purifeed collects nothing, sends nothing, and shares nothing. Everything it does
happens locally in your browser.

## What Purifeed stores

Purifeed saves your filter preferences (which toggles are on or off) using the
browser's built-in extension storage. This data:

- Stays on your device (and syncs through your own browser account if you have
  browser sync enabled).
- Is never transmitted to the developer or any third party.
- Contains only your on/off settings — no personal information, no browsing
  history, no LinkedIn content.

## What Purifeed does not do

- It makes **no network requests** of its own. It does not phone home, load
  remote code, or contact any server.
- It does **not collect, store, or transmit** your LinkedIn data, posts,
  messages, connections, profile, or activity.
- It does **not** use analytics, tracking, advertising, or fingerprinting.
- It does **not** sell or share any data, because it has no data to sell or
  share.

## Permissions and why they exist

- **`storage`** — to remember your filter preferences between sessions.
- **Access to `linkedin.com`** — so the extension can read the feed on the page
  you are viewing and hide the elements you have chosen to hide. This access is
  used only to modify what is shown in your own browser tab. Nothing from the
  page leaves your device.

## Third parties

Purifeed integrates no third-party services. The settings page includes optional
outbound links (a "Buy me a coffee" support link and a "Request a feature" link
to GitHub). These are ordinary links; following them takes you to those sites
under their own privacy policies. Purifeed does not embed their scripts or share
any information with them.
